BTC · Launched 2010-07-13

Overview Bitcoin (BTC) is a cryptocurrency first described in a 2008 whitepaper published under the alias Satoshi Nakamoto, and launched as an operational peer-to-peer digital currency in January 2009. It enables direct value transfer between independent network participants without requiring an intermediary financial institution to approve or facilitate transactions. CoinMarketCap overview Utility and Mechanics BTC transactions are recorded sequentially on a shared public blockchain ledger. Network participants called miners use proof-of-work cryptographic calculations to group pending transactions into new blocks, which are appended to the ledger roughly every 10 minutes. Transaction confirmations increase the certainty that a payment cannot be reversed, and mining participants receive transaction fees along with newly issued bitcoin as compensation for this security work. Official whitepaper Ecosystem and Distinctive Context Bitcoin is the first widely adopted operational cryptocurrency, establishing a decentralized model for peer-to-peer digital value transfer that predates many later blockchain and smart contract platforms. Its design prioritizes neutral, permissionless participation rather than hosting a general-purpose application ecosystem, and its open-source protocol has served as a foundational reference for subsequent digital asset implementations.
